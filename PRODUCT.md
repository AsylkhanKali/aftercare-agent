# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who need help choosing an appropriate type of care and finding nearby clinics, especially when symptoms make the next step feel unclear. The primary task is to describe the situation, understand the level of urgency, compare relevant options, and explicitly approve a supervised test call.

## Product Purpose

AfterCare turns a short symptom description into a safer care-navigation flow without diagnosing, prescribing, or claiming that an appointment exists. Success means the user understands the next step, can inspect sourced clinic options, and stays in control before any external action.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and human. The interface should lower uncertainty while remaining direct about safety limits and prototype boundaries.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a cold hospital portal, an alarmist symptom checker, or a generic AI dashboard. Avoid decorative medical imagery, exaggerated automation claims, and visual treatments that make uncertain recommendations look clinically authoritative.

## Design Principles

1. Put the care task before the technology.
2. Make safety boundaries visible at the moment they matter.
3. Use warmth to reduce anxiety without softening urgent guidance.
4. Keep every external action explicit, reversible where possible, and honestly labeled.
5. Preserve a clear hierarchy from symptom intake to care option to supervised contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard access. Do not rely on color alone for urgency or completion states. Respect reduced-motion preferences, keep form copy plain, and support readable layouts from small phones through desktop screens.
